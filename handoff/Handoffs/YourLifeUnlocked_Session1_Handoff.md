## 2. The Vision

Your Life: Unlocked is a universal life companion app built on a single core promise: it asks very little of you, but delivers intelligent, timely, and meaningful guidance across every dimension of your life — scheduling, relationships, personal growth, and daily habits.

The name signals potential and agency without being prescriptive. It speaks to everyone, regardless of gender, life stage, or relationship status.

---

## 3. The Origin Story

> *"I wonder if there is a way to monetize that knowledge."*
> — The conversation that started it all

The app emerged from a real conversation about the power of personalized intelligence tools. The observation: there is no app that handles life's small but important details intelligently, without burdening the user with endless configuration.

Two perspectives shaped the initial concept:

- A tool that reminds people of the things that matter — oil changes, anniversaries, relationship milestones — without being intrusive
- An app that offers genuine relational intelligence, not generic advice — advice that understands context, timing, and the person receiving it

The founder had recently experienced the online dating world and came away with a visceral understanding of what this app must never become — questionnaires dressed up as self-discovery, depth performed rather than genuine, connection manufactured rather than earned.

That experience became one of the most valuable design constraints in the product's history.

---

## 4. Core Philosophy

### Minimal Input, Maximum Intelligence

The app should feel like a wise friend who just knows — not a form to fill out. Onboarding captures the user's world once, thoughtfully. After that, the app learns through use and time.

### Dual-Perspective Relationship Intelligence

Most apps are siloed: men's apps, women's apps, couple's apps. Your Life: Unlocked speaks to everyone. The intelligence layer adapts — a man and a woman could both receive a nudge about the same anniversary, with framing and advice that resonates differently for each. Smart, not obvious.

### Counter-Cultural Where It Matters

The app takes a deliberate stance against toxic relationship content. It offers men honest, grounded relational advice. It gives women practical intelligence about the dynamics in their lives. It does this without being preachy — through helpfulness, not lectures.

## 5. Founding Principles

These are not preferences. They are guardrails. Every feature, every prompt, every notification gets measured against both of these before it is built.

### The Authenticity Standard

> **No performed depth. No facade of meaning wrapped around superficial data collection.**

The big dating and lifestyle apps have taught users to expect questionnaires dressed up as self-discovery. "What are you passionate about?" "Describe your ideal Sunday." These questions sound profound but are filters in disguise — optimized for engagement metrics, not genuine understanding.

Your Life: Unlocked will never do this. Every question asked must earn its place. If the app can learn something by watching rather than asking, it watches. If it must ask, it asks plainly and only once. The tone is a trusted friend — not a therapist fishing for billable insights.

> *Check: before any onboarding question, feature, or prompt is built, ask — does this feel like something a real person would say, or does it feel like an app trying to seem meaningful?*

### The Restraint Principle

> **Driving engagement is not the goal. The app succeeds when you don't need to open it.**

Every major consumer app is architected to maximize time-on-app. Notifications, streaks, check-ins, badges — all designed to pull you back in. Your Life: Unlocked is built on the opposite philosophy: it helps you when you need it and stays out of the way when you don't.

Success looks like: the nudge landed at the right moment, you acted on it, and you got on with your life. The app never made you feel guilty for not opening it. It never manufactured urgency. It never mistook your attention for its purpose.

> *Check: before any notification, reminder, or re-engagement feature is built, ask — does this serve the user's life, or does it serve the app's metrics?*

## 6. Key Decisions Made

| Decision Area | Decision |
|---|---|
| **App Name** | Your Life: Unlocked |
| **Audience** | Universal — one app for all, not gendered or segmented |
| **Onboarding Model** | Single upfront conversation; app learns the rest through use |
| **Input Philosophy** | Minimal ask from user; intelligent inference does the heavy lifting |
| **Advice Layer** | Dual-perspective: same event, different framing per user |
| **Development Process** | Iterative sessions with written handoffs; no premature coding |
