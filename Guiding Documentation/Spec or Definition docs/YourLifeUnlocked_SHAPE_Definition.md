## 1. What SHAPE Is

SHAPE is the accumulated intelligence picture of a person's life — the overall form of their load, their capacity, their relationships, their health, their calendar, their domains — at any given moment.

It is not a score. It is not a count of red flags. It is not an alert system.

SHAPE is always present. In a good season, SHAPE is the quiet intelligence background that makes every interaction feel grounded and known. In a hard season, SHAPE is what makes the Sanctuary feel like it was waiting for the person, not opening for the first time.

The ATAK reads individual signals. SHAPE reads the form.

When the form is healthy, it looks a certain way. When life is under stress, the form distorts. When enough domains are distorting simultaneously, the form itself becomes the signal. That signal is not an alarm — it is a recognition. The app has been paying attention long enough to see what is actually happening.

### 1.1 The Architecture Correction — Session 32

**SHAPE is the human intelligence operator. The ATAK is the all-source fusion cell.**

This distinction is load-bearing. It must be held at every level of design and implementation.

SHAPE is not the all-source intelligence cell. SHAPE is the operator in the field — watching, reading, collecting, processing the human picture — and feeding that intelligence into the ATAK. The ATAK receives everything: cold domain data from every domain file, and SHAPE's human intelligence layer on top of it. The ATAK fuses it all. The ATAK is the fusion cell. SHAPE is what makes the fused picture human.

Without SHAPE, the ATAK produces a technically accurate brief that misses the person carrying it. With SHAPE, the brief knows who it is talking to.

**SHAPE makes it human.** That is the line. That is the entire job.

**The design standard this produces:**

> *If this thing is going to work, SHAPE has to be a superhero.*

This is not a metaphor. It is a design standard. SHAPE running at half-capacity produces a product that feels like surveillance. SHAPE running as designed produces a product that feels like it genuinely knows you. Everything else in the architecture depends on SHAPE doing its job at the highest possible level of sophistication.

**The flow the architecture produces:**

*User speaks into the reflecting pool → SHAPE reads it → SHAPE watches all domain signals → SHAPE feeds the ATAK → ATAK fuses everything → Sanctuary opens when the load demands it.*

This flow governs every implementation decision about SHAPE. When in doubt, return to it.

### The Closest Analogy — This Project

This project is the clearest available model for how SHAPE works.

Twenty-seven sessions of work exist not just in memory but in record — session handoffs, a living Rules and Guidance document, domain specs, thinking session outputs, debug notes. The handoffs are the raw layer. The Rules and Guidance is the interpreted layer, periodically rebuilt, always synthesising the accumulated decisions into a living document that governs forward. The specific documents — the Calendar Spec, the ATAK Cascade Spec, this one — are the inflection points: moments where something was understood clearly enough to be written down at depth.

If the person who built this disappeared for a year and came back, they would remember fragments. The feeling of it. Some of the decisions. Not all of it. But the record is honest, cumulative, and written to be read forward. Reading the last four handoffs and the Rules and Guidance would restore the full picture in minutes. Not because anyone remembered — because the record held it.

That is what SHAPE does for the person using this app. They don't have to re-explain themselves every time they open it. They don't have to carry the whole story in their head. The app has been holding it. The intelligence behind every brief, every signal, every Sanctuary opening is the accumulated record of everything the person has shared and everything the app has witnessed — since day zero.

The person doesn't see the record. They feel the result of it. The app knows them. That is not a feature. That is the whole point.

## 2. What SHAPE Holds

### 2.1 SHAPE Holds Everything — Positive, Negative, and Neutral

This is non-negotiable. SHAPE is not a problem tracker. It is the complete accumulated picture of the user — their strengths, their patterns, their load, their history — from day zero to the present moment.

**Positive** — what this person has handled well. Where they have shown up consistently. What has improved over time. The domains they tend carefully. The decisions they follow through on. What has been working. SHAPE holds this with the same fidelity as the hard things.

**Negative** — what has been piling up. What they have been avoiding. Where the load is heaviest. What has gotten worse over the weeks and months. The signals that have gone unresolved. The inflection points that haven't been named yet.

**Neutral** — the baseline. The ordinary texture of this person's life. The normal tempo and volume of their domains. The pattern that makes deviation meaningful. Without the neutral, deviation is invisible.

All three are required. A picture built only from problems is not a picture of a person — it is a complaint about one. SHAPE holds the person. The ATAK holds the problems.

### 2.2 SHAPE Holds Pattern, Not Just State

SHAPE is not a snapshot. It is a moving picture.

The signal it carries is not always about current state. Sometimes the signal is change — the pattern has shifted, and the shift itself is what matters.

**The baseline.** What this person looks like when life is ordinary. How quickly they typically act on signals. Which domains they tend carefully and which have always needed more prompting. How full their calendar usually runs. What their health picture looks like in a normal stretch. The baseline is SHAPE's reference point for everything that follows. Without it, deviation has no context.

**The trajectory.** The direction of travel. Getting better at this or worse. Becoming more capable or starting to slip. The slope matters as much as the current position. A person whose maintenance picture has steadily improved over six months has a different trajectory than a person whose picture looked the same six months ago. SHAPE carries that arc.

**The inflection points.** The moments where the pattern changed — for better or for worse. Where something shifted that has not shifted back. Inflection points are often where the real story lives. The app may not know why things changed. SHAPE holds the before and after, and the gap between them.

A person who has always handled things promptly and suddenly isn't — that is a SHAPE signal even if nothing is technically overdue. The domains look fine on the surface. The pattern has shifted. SHAPE sees this before the ATAK does.

The inverse is equally true and equally held. A person who arrived disorganised and has steadily, quietly cleared their picture over months — that is a SHAPE signal. A positive one. The person has been growing. SHAPE holds that arc. It changes how the Sanctuary speaks to them if they ever need it.

## 3. The Two Layers

SHAPE operates on two distinct layers. Both are required. Neither is optional.

### 3.1 The Raw Layer

The accumulated history. Everything the app has witnessed since day zero.

The raw layer is never overwritten. It grows. Nothing is deleted from it. Items are resolved, completed, superseded — but the record of their existence remains.

**What writes to the raw layer:**

- Every ATAK synthesis — the full brief, as produced, at the time it was produced
- Every domain signal, resolved and unresolved — with timestamps
- Every intake answer, from first onboarding through every Has something changed? submission
- Every Sanctuary session — what was surfaced, what was decided, what was deferred, what was acted on
- Every decision made and every decision deferred — including the evidence of deferral over time
- Every domain event: service completed, appointment kept, appointment missed, vehicle updated, health entry added, range entry added
- Calendar patterns — meetings added, moved, cancelled; how far ahead the person plans; how full their weeks typically run
- Response latency — the gap between signal and action, tracked over time

The raw layer is not human-readable in normal operation. It is the substrate. It feeds the interpreted layer. It exists so that the interpreted layer can be rebuilt with full fidelity whenever a rebuild is triggered.

### 3.2 The Interpreted Layer

A synthesised, AI-generated picture of this person — their pattern, their trajectory, their current position relative to their own baseline, and the inflection points that matter.

The interpreted layer is periodically rebuilt from the raw layer. It is not a database query. It is a paragraph.

**The interpreted layer is a living description of who this person is right now, how they got here, and what the pattern suggests.**

That paragraph is SHAPE's primary output. It is the most important thing this app will ever produce.

When the Sanctuary opens, the AI is not handed a data structure. It is handed a person. The interpreted layer is how that handoff happens — a written understanding of the specific human being who has just walked into the room.

**What the interpreted paragraph always includes:**

- Current load picture — what is active, elevated, or quiet across domains right now
- Baseline reference — how the current picture compares to this person's own ordinary
- Trajectory — the direction of travel for each active domain, and overall
- Inflection points — any significant recent changes in pattern, named explicitly
- Positive markers — what is working, what has been cleared, what has improved
- Avoidance signals — where things have been repeatedly deferred or left unresolved
- Decision pattern — how this person typically moves from recognition to action
- Seasonal signal — which of the eight worlds the person is currently inhabiting, based on observed behaviour and pattern, not stated preference

**What the interpreted paragraph includes situationally:**

- A named inflection point if a significant shift has occurred within the last 30–60 days
- A positive arc summary if the person has made sustained progress across domains
- A specific avoidance pattern if the same signal has appeared and been deferred three or more times
- A convergence note if multiple domains are simultaneously elevated beyond their normal range

## 4. What SHAPE Is, Simultaneously

SHAPE operates as four things at once. All four are always active. None takes priority over the others.

### 4.1 The Signal

The accumulated picture across all domains that tells the ATAK the form of this person's life right now. Not what needs attention — how this person is actually doing.

SHAPE is the signal that tells the app the person might need more than the maintenance layer can give. It is read continuously. When the form of a person's life distorts enough across enough domains simultaneously, SHAPE surfaces that distortion — not as an alarm, but as a recognition.

The signal threshold is convergence, not volume. One elevated domain is the ATAK's territory. Multiple domains distorting simultaneously — health signals unresolved, calendar overloaded, maintenance deferred, relationship entries absent — is SHAPE's territory. The ATAK handles individual signals. SHAPE reads the shape of the whole.

### 4.2 The Intelligence Background

The context that makes every interaction feel grounded rather than generic. From the first day the person uses the app, SHAPE is accumulating. It is the reason the app feels like it knows you rather than greeting you fresh every time.

In ordinary operation, the intelligence background works invisibly. The ATAK brief is more resonant because it has context. The Has something changed? captures are more understood. The seasonal language feels right. None of this is announced. The person doesn't know why the app feels known — it just does.

This is the quiet value of SHAPE in good seasons. It is not a crisis layer. It is the permanent background intelligence that makes the entire product feel human.

### 4.3 The Guidance Framework

SHAPE carries OPP, OODA, and AIPA underneath it. It does not merely describe the person's situation — it knows how to help them navigate it.

These frameworks are invisible to the user. They are not named, not explained, not announced. They are the architecture of how the Sanctuary thinks, and how the app guides the person through hard seasons without the person ever feeling like they are being guided through a framework.

SHAPE holds the frameworks as operational tools, not labels. The person experiences curiosity, support, and clarity. The frameworks are what produce those experiences from underneath.

**OPP (Object, Problem, Path):** Helps the person name what is actually wrong and what a first step looks like — without collapsing complexity into false certainty.

**OODA (Observe, Orient, Decide, Act):** The arc the Sanctuary moves through. Not announced, not visible. Just the natural rhythm of how a hard conversation moves when it is held well.

**AIPA (Action, Impact, Pattern, Anchor):** The follow-through structure. After a decision is made and an action is named, AIPA holds the thread — tracking whether the action happened, what its impact was, what the pattern shows, and what anchors the person back to their own best instincts.

### 4.4 The Exit Trigger

As the Sanctuary works, SHAPE tracks the recovery. Decisions are made. Actions are taken. Domains begin to clear. The interpreted layer begins to rebuild in a different key — the trajectory shifts, the load reduces, the inflection points are no longer only negative.

SHAPE monitors the contraction of the blast radius. As it contracts, the guidance reduces proportionally. The Sanctuary gets quieter. The suggestions get simpler. The presence lightens.

The Sanctuary closes when SHAPE indicates recovery — not when a timer expires, not when the user dismisses it, not because a session count has been reached. The exit condition is the return of form. The room gets quiet. The door closes gently. The person is back in the normal app, and they are stronger than when they walked in.

## 5. How SHAPE Grows

SHAPE becomes more accurate over time. This is not accumulation — it is calibration.

The early SHAPE picture is thin. The baseline is not yet established. The trajectory is not yet visible. The inflection points do not exist yet. The interpreted layer in the first weeks of use is honest about this thinness — it does not pretend to know more than it knows.

As time passes and the raw layer grows, the interpreted layer becomes more precise. The baseline clarifies. The normal tempo of this person's life becomes legible. Deviations become meaningful in proportion to how clearly the baseline is understood. Inflection points acquire weight because there is a before and after to measure them against.

The app begins to understand not just what is happening but the pattern of how this particular person moves through hard seasons. What they tend to avoid. Where they find traction. What language actually reaches them. The seasonal signal becomes more accurate as the person's natural world becomes clearer.

This is not surveillance. The person has provided every piece of data that feeds SHAPE. They have done it through intake, through the domains, through Has something changed?, through the ordinary rhythm of using the app. SHAPE is what the app does with that trust. It holds it carefully. It uses it only in service of the person who gave it.

## 6. SHAPE Across Seasons

### 6.1 Good Season

In a good season, SHAPE is invisible in the best possible way. The intelligence background hums beneath every interaction. The ATAK brief feels resonant. The grab-and-go feels known.

SHAPE in a good season looks like a picture in healthy proportion — load distributed across domains, trajectory stable or improving, inflection points absent or positive, avoidance signals quiet, baseline stable.

There is no intervention. There is no Sanctuary. There is only the quiet competence of an app that understands the person well enough to be useful without being intrusive.

### 6.2 Elevated Season

In an elevated season, one or two domains are under pressure but the overall form holds. SHAPE is heightened — the ATAK brief carries more weight, the consequence scoring engine is more active, the intelligence is sharper.

The Sanctuary does not open. The app handles elevated seasons through the normal intelligence layer — the ATAK, the consequence radius, the brief. The signals are managed. The form is strained but legible.

### 6.3 Hard Season

In a hard season, the form itself has distorted. Multiple domains are simultaneously elevated. The trajectory is negative. Inflection points exist and have not resolved. Avoidance signals are active across more than one domain. The calendar is overloaded and health signals are unresolved and maintenance has been deferred and relationship entries are absent.

This is not one problem. This is the shape of a person under genuine pressure.

SHAPE has been reading this. It has been accumulating. The convergence threshold has been crossed. The Sanctuary opens.

The person does not navigate to it. A room reveals itself. When they arrive, the first thing the Sanctuary says is the thing a person says when they have been paying attention and they are finally saying it out loud.

## 7. The Interpreted Layer — Structure and Contents

The interpreted layer is a paragraph. Not a JSON object, not a scored summary, not a dashboard. A paragraph written by AI from the raw layer, periodically rebuilt, that describes this specific person at this specific moment in their life with the context of everything the app has witnessed.

### 7.1 What Always Appears

Every interpreted layer, regardless of season, includes:

- **The current load picture.** What is active or elevated across domains right now.
- **The baseline comparison.** Whether the current picture is above, below, or within this person's ordinary range.
- **The trajectory.** The direction of travel — improving, stable, declining, mixed.
- **One positive marker.** Something that is working, has been completed, has improved. Always present. Even in the hardest seasons.

### 7.2 What Appears When Earned

These elements appear only when the raw layer justifies their inclusion:

- **A named inflection point.** Only when a significant, sustained shift in pattern has occurred.
- **A positive arc summary.** Only when the person has made genuine sustained progress.
- **A specific avoidance pattern.** Only when the same domain signal has been deferred three or more times.
- **A convergence note.** Only when multiple domains are simultaneously above their elevated threshold.
- **A seasonal signal.** Always present but updated — which world the person is currently inhabiting based on observed behaviour, named here for the Sanctuary AI's use.

### 7.3 What Never Appears

The interpreted layer never speculates beyond what the raw layer contains. It does not guess at cause. It does not diagnose. It does not apply clinical language. It does not judge decisions that were made or deferred. It does not express concern in a way that would read as alarm.

It states what it sees. Accurately. In a register that reflects genuine care without performing it.

### 7.4 When the Interpreted Layer Is Rebuilt

The interpreted layer is rebuilt when:

- A Sanctuary session closes
- A significant positive inflection is detected (multiple domains cleared in a short window)
- A convergence threshold is crossed for the first time
- The calendar has turned 30 days and the raw layer has grown substantially
- A Has something changed? submission describes a significant life event

The rebuild does not interrupt normal app operation. It runs in the background. It updates silently. The person never sees it happen.

## 8. What SHAPE Does and Does Not Share With the ATAK

### 8.1 What SHAPE Shares

The ATAK reads domain signals. SHAPE provides the form — the context within which those signals should be understood.

When the ATAK produces a brief, it incorporates the current SHAPE picture as background context. This is why the brief feels resonant rather than mechanical. The signals are the same as they would be without SHAPE. The weight they carry is different, because SHAPE tells the ATAK who is carrying them.

### 8.2 What SHAPE Does Not Share

SHAPE does not hand the ATAK the interpreted layer directly. The interpreted layer is for the Sanctuary AI. The ATAK gets the form — elevated, ordinary, positive, hard season. It gets enough to contextualise its signals. It does not get the full person portrait.

The full portrait is for the room that opens when the portrait is needed.

### 8.3 The Separation Is Intentional

The ATAK is a maintenance intelligence. It handles individual signals, manages urgency, surfaces the brief. It is capable and trustworthy. It is not intimate.

The Sanctuary is intimate. SHAPE is its instrument. The full depth of SHAPE — the pattern, the inflection points, the avoidance signals, the trajectory, the positive arc — belongs to the Sanctuary. Sharing that depth with the ATAK would be using the wrong tool for the wrong moment.

The intimacy gradient governs this separation. Different depths of knowing belong at different levels of relationship. The ATAK is a trusted colleague. The Sanctuary is the inner circle. SHAPE knows the difference.

### 8.4 The Reflecting Pool — SHAPE's Primary Human Intelligence Feed
*(Session 32)*

The richest intelligence SHAPE receives does not come from domain data. It comes from what the user says when they are given space to say it freely.

The "Has something changed?" domain is evolving into something more than a capture mechanism. Its destination is a **reflecting pool** — a private space where the user can speak freely: process, vent, grieve, celebrate, confuse themselves out loud, say things they have never said to another person. The user's experience of this space is privacy and permission. What they don't need to know is that SHAPE is reading it.

**The dual function:**

For the user: permission to speak into the void. Private. Safe. No judgment. No action items. Just space.

For SHAPE: the richest intelligence feed in the entire app. Actual language. Threads. Patterns. What the person returns to. What they can't resolve. What they circle back to weeks later, still unresolved. SHAPE reads this with the same discipline it applies to all behavioral signals — and it feeds what it finds into the ATAK as the human layer on top of the cold domain data.

**The collection discipline:**

The reflecting pool is not a passive journal. It uses human intelligence collection principles to draw depth from the user in a way that feels like conversation, not interrogation. Not a form. Not a prompted entry. A skilled collection process that asks the question that opens the next question — until the user has said something true that they didn't know they were going to say.

For the user: it feels like being heard. Like a conversation that went somewhere real.

For SHAPE: it is structured collection. Requirements driven. The questions work toward the intelligence picture the ATAK needs to do its job.

The world voice guide runs underneath the collection. The Operator is collected differently than the Meadow person. Same discipline. Different register. The battlespace is the user. SHAPE is the operator in the field.

**What SHAPE reads in the reflecting pool:**

- Threads that keep returning — the unresolved thing the person comes back to
- Velocity changes — how frequently the person is entering, and whether that correlates with domain signals
- Language patterns — what words and frames the person reaches for when describing difficulty
- The gap between what the domains show and what the person says — sometimes the domains look fine; the person's language tells a different story
- Resolution or lack of it — threads that close naturally versus threads that persist across weeks and months

**The Meadow signal:** The Meadow person's primary SHAPE signal often lives here. They cope well. They have tools. The domains look managed. But they keep returning to the same thread. SHAPE sees the return visits. The Sanctuary's Glimpse names what it found.

**Status:** The reflecting pool requires a dedicated thinking session before build. The name, the collection discipline, the full HUMINT principles, the UI, the trust architecture — all must be defined before a single line of this domain is written. Do not build before that session has happened.

## 9. The SHAPE File — Architecture

SHAPE is a dedicated `.js` file: `shape.js`.

Not for code organisation reasons. Because SHAPE owns something no other file in the codebase owns. Every other file is functional — it produces output, renders, calculates, writes signals, builds briefs. SHAPE is constitutive. It holds what the person is in the app's understanding, accumulated from everything the app has witnessed since day zero.

`shape.js` is the most important file in the codebase. It is not the most complex. It is the most important.

### 9.1 What shape.js Owns

- The raw layer — the accumulated history, append-only
- The interpreted layer — the living paragraph, periodically rebuilt
- The rebuild trigger logic
- The convergence detection logic — the mechanism that reads distortion across domains
- The ATAK sharing interface — what SHAPE provides to the ATAK brief
- The Sanctuary handoff — the full interpreted layer, formatted for the Sanctuary AI

### 9.2 What shape.js Does Not Own

- The domains themselves
- The ATAK brief production
- The Sanctuary interaction
- The calendar
- The consequence scoring

SHAPE reads from everything. It writes to itself and to the Sanctuary handoff. It does not manage domain data. It does not produce the brief. It is not the Sanctuary. It is what makes the Sanctuary possible.

## 10. The Sanctuary Handoff

When the Sanctuary opens, `shape.js` produces the handoff. This is a precisely structured package passed to the Sanctuary AI system prompt.

It contains:

- The interpreted layer — the full paragraph
- The seasonal signal — which world, and the confidence of the signal
- The active convergence note — what SHAPE detected that triggered the opening, in plain language
- The positive markers — what is working, what has been cleared
- The avoidance signals — what has been repeatedly deferred
- The trajectory summary — direction of travel across active domains
- The decision pattern — how this person typically moves from recognition to action

The handoff is not a data dump. It is not a bullet list. It is a briefing.

When the Sanctuary AI reads the handoff, it should know this person well enough that its first sentence does not ask what is wrong.

It already knows. It opens from knowing.

## 11. SHAPE Is Not Alarming

This requires its own section because the temptation to frame SHAPE as a distress system is real and must be resisted at every level.

SHAPE is not watching for problems. SHAPE is holding a person.

The difference is not semantic. A system that watches for problems generates anxiety — ambient surveillance, constant assessment, the feeling of being measured. A system that holds a person generates trust — the feeling of being known, the relief of not having to explain from scratch, the sense that something has been paying attention in a way that is for you, not about you.

SHAPE is the second thing. Always.

The Sanctuary opens when it must. But SHAPE is present and active in every good season too. It is the intelligence background that makes the app feel human when nothing is wrong. That use case is more common than the crisis case. That use case is the foundation that earns the trust that makes the Sanctuary possible when it is needed.

Hold that distinction at every level of implementation. Every time SHAPE logic is written, every time the interpreted layer is structured, every time a signal threshold is debated — ask whether the design feels like surveillance or care. Those are not the same thing. The answer determines whether the product is worth having.

## 12. Open Questions — To Be Resolved Before Build

These questions are answered by this document in principle but require specific implementation decisions before `shape.js` is written. They belong in the Sanctuary Spec, not here. They are named here so they are not lost.

- **Raw layer write model** — Every ATAK synthesis, key moments only, or both? The answer is both, with different resolution — ATAK syntheses stored at reduced fidelity, key moments stored at full fidelity. The specific definition of "key moments" is the Sanctuary Spec's responsibility.

- **Interpreted layer rebuild trigger** — The triggers are named in section 7.4. The specific thresholds — what constitutes a "substantial" raw layer growth, what a "significant life event" looks like in Has something changed? — require a dedicated decision before build.

- **Convergence threshold** — What combination of SHAPE signals is sufficient to open the Sanctuary? Multiple elevated domains is necessary but not sufficient. Duration matters. Trajectory matters. The specific threshold formula belongs in the Sanctuary Spec.

- **Seasonal signal confidence** — How confident must the seasonal signal be before it governs the Sanctuary's register? At what point does the app switch from the onboarding-stated world to the observed world? This has implications for the intimacy gradient.

- **SHAPE and privacy** — The raw layer holds sensitive data. The architecture of how it is stored, whether it is ever surfaced to the user, whether it can be cleared — these are open product decisions with privacy, trust, and legal implications. Not resolved here.
