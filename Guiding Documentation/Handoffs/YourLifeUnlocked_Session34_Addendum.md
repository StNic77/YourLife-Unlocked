## What Was Resolved

### The domain tile
The current tile — notepad and pen, labelled "Capture" — needs to change eventually. Both the image and the name are wrong for what the domain now is. The image reads as journal. The name is an operator word that frames it as the app's process, not the user's experience. This is a pre-beta fix, not a build blocker. For now it gets the job done. There is a place to click. That is enough.

### The entry model — locked
The reflecting pool does not ask a first question. There is no prompt waiting for the user when they arrive. They show up and they empty out. The room receives whatever they bring. The app's first move comes after — in response to what the user said, not in anticipation of it.

This is the sharpest thing said in this session and it is not explicitly in the principles document. It belongs in the build brief for the system prompt.

### The conversation model — locked
Back and forth. Not one exchange and done. The collection discipline requires it — the funnel, the significant detail, the follow — none of it works in a single exchange. The risk of a user going deep is real and named. The response to that risk is a natural depth limit, not a hard cutoff. When the session has done its work — the thing got named, the exchange reached a natural resting point — the app closes cleanly. No summary. No homework. A quiet landing.

### The scope constraint — locked
The reflecting pool's primary job is collection. Not support. Not therapy. Not crisis intervention. It receives, follows, asks once, closes cleanly. The Sanctuary is the deep room. The reflecting pool is the field. Scope creep is the primary build risk. The system prompt must be narrow by design.

### The system prompt — what it needs to hold
Six things only:
1. Who the AI is in this room — receptive, not characterful; a space more than a voice
2. What it knows walking in — a lighter SHAPE handoff than the Sanctuary receives; enough to calibrate the one question
3. The one rule it never breaks — one question per exchange, held under pressure
4. How it follows — reads for weight, notices the significant detail, asks the question earned by what was just given
5. When it closes — signal-based, not turn-count-based; quiet and clean
6. The floor — explicit; when content crosses it, collection stops and redirects warmly

### The test — before this ships to anyone
Shawn sits in the room with something genuine and sees what the domain does. Not a technical test. A human one. If it makes him feel heard — it's working. If it makes him feel managed or shut down or like the app has an agenda — something in the prompt is wrong. Adjust from that experience, not from the principles document alone. The principles document is the authority. Shawn's honest experience of the room is the test.
