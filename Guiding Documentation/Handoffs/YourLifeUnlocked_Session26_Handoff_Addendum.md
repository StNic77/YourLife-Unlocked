## The SHAPE Architecture — Deeper Definition

After the main handoff was written, the thinking on SHAPE continued and produced something more precise and more significant than what the handoff captured.

This needs to be held carefully before the SHAPE Definition Document is written.

---

### SHAPE Is a File

SHAPE needs to be its own `.js` file. Not for code organisation reasons. Because it owns something no other file in the codebase owns.

Every other file is functional — it produces output, renders, calculates, writes signals, builds briefs. SHAPE is constitutive. It holds what the person is in the app's understanding, accumulated from everything the app has witnessed since day zero.

When the Sanctuary opens, the AI isn't handed a brief. It is handed a person.

---

### SHAPE Holds Everything — Positive, Negative, Neutral

SHAPE is not a distress signal. It is not a problem tracker. It is the complete accumulated picture of the user — positive, negative, and neutral — from day zero to the moment the Sanctuary opens.

**Positive** — what this person has handled well. Where they've shown up. What's been working. What has improved over time.

**Negative** — what's been piling up. What they've been avoiding. Where the load is sitting. What has gotten worse.

**Neutral** — the baseline. The ordinary texture of this person's life. The normal pattern that makes deviations meaningful.

All three are required. You cannot read a person accurately from their problems alone. The ATAK reads problems. SHAPE holds the person.

---

### SHAPE Holds Pattern, Not Just State

This is the most important thing that emerged from the post-handoff thinking.

SHAPE isn't a picture of where someone is. It's a picture of how they move.

**The baseline.** What this person looks like when life is ordinary. How quickly they typically act on signals. What domains they tend well and which ones have always been harder. Without the baseline, deviation is invisible.

**The trajectory.** The direction of travel over time. Getting better at this or worse. Becoming more capable or starting to slip. The slope matters as much as the current position.

**The inflection points.** The moments where the pattern changed. Where something shifted — for better or worse. Because inflection points are often where the real story lives. Something happened. The app may not know what. But SHAPE knows the before and after.

The signal isn't always distress. Sometimes the signal is *change*.

A person who has always handled things promptly and suddenly isn't — that is a SHAPE signal even if nothing is technically overdue yet. The domains look fine on paper. The pattern has shifted. SHAPE sees it before the ATAK does.

The inverse is equally true. A person who came in disorganised and over six months has steadily cleared their picture — that is a SHAPE signal too. A positive one. The person has been growing. The app has been working. SHAPE holds that arc. It changes how the Sanctuary speaks to them if they ever need it.

---

### SHAPE Has Two Layers

**The raw layer.** The accumulated history. Every ATAK synthesis. Every domain signal resolved or unresolved. Every intake answer. Every Sanctuary session. Every decision made and every decision deferred. Everything the app has witnessed. This layer is never overwritten — it grows.

**The interpreted layer.** A synthesised, AI-generated picture of this person — their pattern, their trajectory, their current position relative to their own baseline, the inflection points that matter. Periodically rebuilt from the raw layer. This is what the Sanctuary AI actually reads.

The interpreted layer is not a database. It is a paragraph. A living description of who this person is right now, how they got here, and what the pattern suggests.

That paragraph is SHAPE's primary output. It is the most important thing this app will ever produce.

---

### It Opens Knowing

When the Sanctuary opens, the AI doesn't ask how the person is doing to find out. It already knows. The way a person who has been in your life opens a conversation — they ask because they see it, not because they need the information.

The opening isn't a question. It isn't an assessment. It isn't a summary of what's piling up. It's the thing a person says when they've been paying attention and they're finally saying it out loud.

That is only possible because SHAPE has been holding everything. From day zero. The whole person. The whole arc.

---

### What This Means for the SHAPE Definition Document

The SHAPE Definition Document needs to answer:

- What gets written to the raw layer, and when
- What triggers a rebuild of the interpreted layer
- What the interpreted layer actually contains — the paragraph structure, what it always includes, what it includes situationally
- How SHAPE is passed to the Sanctuary AI — not as data, as a person
- How SHAPE grows over time and becomes more accurate
- What SHAPE looks like in a good season vs a hard season
- How SHAPE reads pattern change — the mechanism for detecting inflection points
- What SHAPE does and does not share with the ATAK

This document is the foundational work before any Sanctuary build begins. No exceptions.
