## 2. Decisions Locked This Session

### The App Name — Typographic Treatment

The colon in "Your Life: Unlocked" has been retired. The name is now rendered as:

> **Your Life / Unlocked**

The slash is not a line break. It is intentional punctuation — a character that is part of the name. It sits at full weight alongside the words. It does not apologise for itself.

Typography locked: Playfair Display, 500 weight, letter-spacing 0.05em. The slash inherits the same treatment as the surrounding words.

---

### The Tagline — "Life, tended."

The placeholder line "Everything in its season" has been retired permanently. It was atmospheric without being useful. The replacement:

> **Life, tended.**

Two words. The comma does real work — it is a pause, a breath, a gentle assertion. It speaks to every path simultaneously without belonging to any one of them. It is the soul of the product in the smallest possible space.

Typography locked: Lora italic, 300 weight, letter-spacing 0.06em. A different face from the title — quieter, softer, as if spoken after the title was said aloud.

---

### The Welcome Screen — Layout

Text sits bottom-left. The image owns the rest of the frame. The expanse does the emotional work. The words land quietly in the corner like a caption on something vast.

The full welcome screen reads, from bottom-left:

> Your Life / Unlocked
> Life, tended.
> begin

"begin" remains in spaced caps, quiet, subordinate to everything above it.

This layout is locked in direction. Fine-tuning of vertical position and edge padding happens in the build.

### The Bridge Question

The previous line — *"Where do you feel most like yourself?"* — was doing the right intelligence work but landing with too much weight. It felt like an assessment. The replacement:

> **"Find somewhere that feels like you."**

This reframes the gallery entirely. It is not a question about identity with a right answer. It is an invitation to look until something feels familiar. The person is not being asked who they are. They are being asked to recognise something when they see it.

The intelligence is identical. The pressure is gone.

### The Enter Button

Already agreed in Session 6b, confirmed here: the button copy changes from "enter this world" to **"start here."** The choice is a beginning, not a verdict.

### The Eight Arrival Lines — All Locked

The arrival lines were completely reconceived this session. They are no longer welcome statements or atmospheric descriptions of each world. They are the whisper between curiosity and commitment — the single line that makes it safe to take the step.

Each line is calibrated to the emotional posture of the person already standing at that world:

| Path | Arrival Line |
|---|---|
| **Operator** | *"First things first."* |
| **Range** | *"The gate's open."* |
| **Garden** | *"Let's grow something good together."* |
| **Journey** | *"You've already started."* |
| **Playbook** | *"You just made a smart call."* |
| **Summit** | *"Let's climb."* |
| **Practice** | *"Let's hit the mat."* |
| **Meadow** | *"Your peace, tended."* |

The philosophy behind the set:

- **Stoic / Grounded** (Operator, Range) — the line confirms rather than reassures. They've already decided. The line says *yes, this is the place.*
- **Nurturing / Connected** (Garden, Journey) — the line gently removes the stakes. Warm without being effusive. *You don't have to have this figured out.*
- **Achievement / Oriented** (Playbook, Summit) — the line frames the choice as the first smart move. Beginning, not arriving.
- **Spiritual / Philosophical** (Practice, Meadow) — the line barely speaks. The image has done everything. The words just settle.

Note: all eight lines remain open to revision. The sentiment is locked. The exact wording can be revisited if something sharper surfaces.

## 3. The New Principle — The Feather Rule

> **Every line of copy in this product is a feather doing the work of a hand.**

This entered the record during the arrival line workshop and applies to everything downstream — every nudge, every check-in, every piece of intelligence the app surfaces. The weight is never felt. The effect is real.

The arrival lines are not just gallery copy. They are the first note of a voice that never changes. Every nudge the Operator receives should be recognisable as coming from the same place as *"First things first."* Every moment the Meadow person is tended should carry the same quality as *"Your peace, tended."*

If a nudge, notification, or check-in couldn't have been written by the world it comes from — it goes.

---

## 4. The Back Door — Decided in Principle, Not Yet Built

A person browsing the gallery must be able to step back at any moment without shame. The app never traps. The app never pressures. The ability to retreat is part of the tending philosophy.

The back door must be:

- **Always present** — visible at every point in the gallery experience
- **Non-threatening** — not labelled "go back" or "cancel," which implies they did something wrong
- **Welcoming** — the act of stepping back is treated as completely natural
- **Carrying gravitas** — considered, not an escape hatch

The exact copy and UI treatment for the back door is a Session 8 workshop item. The principle is locked: the person is always in command. The app never makes them feel committed before they are ready.

This is the Restraint Principle at its most literal. The app does not hold on.

## 5. What This Session Confirmed

Two people independently landed on the same lines — *"You just made a smart call"* for Playbook, *"Let's grow something good together"* for Garden — without prompting. This is the Cheesy Hook Check operating as intended: when a line is right, it is findable by anyone thinking clearly about the world it serves.

The Meadow line — *"Your peace, tended."* — emerged from the founder's own language. It echoes *"Life, tended."* deliberately. The product's tagline and its most personal world now share a voice. That is not an accident. That is the product knowing itself.

The insight that confirmed the arrival line philosophy: the same intelligence, delivered in eight completely different voices, producing the same outcome for every person who receives it. Two users will never have the same experience of this product. Both will feel tended.

## 6. Founding Principles — Carried Forward

**The Authenticity Standard** *(Session 1)*
> No performed depth. No facade of meaning wrapped around superficial data collection. Every question must earn its place. If the app can learn by watching, it watches. If it must ask, it asks plainly and only once.
> *Check: does this feel like something a real person would say, or an app trying to seem meaningful?*

**The Restraint Principle** *(Session 1)*
> Driving engagement is not the goal. The app succeeds when you don't need to open it.
> *Check: does this serve the user's life, or does it serve the app's metrics?*

**The Tending Philosophy** *(Session 3)*
> This app is not in the extraction business. It tends. It notices. It shows up at the right moment and retreats when its work is done.
> *Check: does this feature take from the user, or give to them?*

**The Seasonal Intelligence Principle** *(Session 4)*
> Analogies are seasons, not identities. The app learns which season the user is in and adapts invisibly.
> *Check: are we serving the user's current season, or the identity they presented at onboarding?*

**The Cheesy Hook Check** *(Session 5)*
> Before any analogy-specific language is locked — ask: does this sound like an app trying to seem like this world, or someone who actually lives in it?
> *Check: would a real inhabitant of this world find this embarrassing? If yes, it goes.*

**The Feather Rule** *(Session 7)*
> Every line of copy in this product is a feather doing the work of a hand. The weight is never felt. The effect is real.
> *Check: does this line do its work without the user feeling it land?*
