## 1. What This Session Was

No code was written. One significant document was produced.

It was earned the same way the Sanctuary Prompt Brief was earned in Session 30: conversation before document. Shawn held each world in his own words before a line was written.

---

## 2. The Governing Principle — Locked

> **Plain language for everyone. Moderated by the world.**

Not a different language per world. The same language — turned up or down, sharpened or softened, quickened or slowed. This principle governs every piece of copy in the product from this session forward.

Three tests locked alongside it:

**The trope test:** Does this copy require the costume to make sense? If yes, it goes. The aesthetic is the bait. The voice lives beneath it — in how that world's people actually carry themselves, process difficulty, and receive care.

**The feather test:** Does the line announce itself? If it lands with a thud instead of a settle, rewrite it.

**The Betty test:** Could this voice reach someone the world image wasn't designed for? The Garden doesn't brief Betty. It tends her. The architecture is identical underneath. The surface is completely different.

---

## 3. How the Session Ran

Each world was earned through conversation. Shawn was asked one question before anything was written: *when you picture someone in this world in a genuinely hard season — who are you seeing?* The answer led to the source material. The source material led to the voice. The voice led to the entry.

The worlds closest to Shawn's lived experience came fastest and ran deepest:

- **The Range** — came from Shawn's father. A stoic Alberta farmer whose difficulty was impossibly quiet. The entry took one conversation to find.
- **The Operator** — came from a real deployment. A four-man team in a denied environment, a young family at home, a roof coming off in a storm, a bad boss. Dark humour as the differentiator from Range. Named and locked.
- **The Meadow** — came from a breakup Shawn worked through, and a dear friend who can see his lake but can't find the trail. Peace earned through struggle, not found. The Meadow is void of noise.
- **The Practice** — came from Shawn's own mountain biking. Two hours of zen hardship. The line on the trail. The only thing that matters is the line. Everything else shrinks.

The worlds farthest from Shawn's lived experience required more questions and more time:

- **The Garden** — came from his grandmother and his ex mother-in-law. Women who gave and contorted and were always last. The guilt of receiving named as the key that unlocks the world.
- **The Journey** — resolved into two versions: running toward oneself, running away from oneself. The shadow lives inside the motion. SHAPE watches for the flip.
- **The Playbook** — came from watching someone clear a room without knowing it. The scoreboard that follows them everywhere. The room that empties gradually. Loneliness as the hard season.
- **The Summit** — came from the logic of arrival. Two Summit people end up in the Sanctuary: the one who reached the peak and felt nothing, and the one who got there and had nobody to share it with. The one doing it right never sees the Sanctuary.

## 4. Decisions Locked This Session

- World Voice Guide governing principle locked: plain language for everyone, moderated by the world
- Three tests locked: trope test, feather test, Betty test
- All eight world entries written and locked as pointing true — subject to polish pass in context, not before
- Standing rule locked: every piece of copy that enters this product is measured against this guide before it ships. World by world. No exceptions. Brutally and honestly.
- Operator polish pass confirmed: flagged, deferred until all eight worlds are visible together. The entry in the guide is the standard the existing copy gets measured against.
- The conversation-before-the-document approach confirmed as the right method for sessions at this depth

**Key distinctions earned this session:**

- **Range vs Operator:** Same stoic posture, different release valve. The Range absorbs in silence. The Operator finds the dark humour. Both name the thing and move — but the Operator has to function inside the weight, not just carry it.
- **Meadow vs Practice:** The Meadow person finds peace. The Practice person makes it. The Meadow is the lake. The Practice is the ride to get there.
- **Playbook vs Summit:** The Playbook needs to beat someone. The Summit needs to climb something. Similar cost to the people around them. Different engine underneath.
- **Journey — two versions:** Running toward oneself (healthy). Running away from oneself (shadow). The same stride. SHAPE watches for which engine is running.
