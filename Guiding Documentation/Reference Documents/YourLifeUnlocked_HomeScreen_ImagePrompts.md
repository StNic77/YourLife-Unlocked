## GLOBAL INSTRUCTION SET
*Apply to every image in this set. Paste this before each individual prompt.*

Photorealistic interior scene, portrait orientation 9:16, ultra high resolution. Cinematic lighting — practical light sources only, no studio lighting. The scene is a personal space that belongs to one specific kind of person. It is lived in but not messy. Purposeful. Everything in frame has a reason to be there.

**Mood:** Still, pre-mission. The owner has stepped away but will return. The space is ready.

**Lighting:** Low, warm, directional. Single dominant light source — a lamp, a window, a screen glow. Deep shadows. Rich midtones. No overexposed highlights.

**Colour:** Desaturated but not cold. Muted earth tones, worn materials, aged surfaces. Nothing synthetic or bright. Film grain present.

**Composition:** The primary interface object — the one this person would reach for first — is prominent, slightly off-centre, lit. It draws the eye immediately. Supporting objects are present in the scene but peripheral, naturally placed. No forced symmetry.

**Critical constraints:**
- No readable text of any kind — no labels, no writing visible enough to read, no brand names
- No people, no faces, no hands, no body parts
- No weapons of any kind — no firearms, no blades on display
- No phone screens showing real UI
- The scene must feel like a real place, not a product shoot
- Avoid anything that reads as stock photography

**Format:** Same visual treatment as the existing gallery images for this app — atmospheric, world-specific, photographic, not illustrative.

## THE EIGHT ROOMS

---

### 1. THE OPERATOR
**Filename:** `OPERATOR_HOME.png`

A well-appointed tactical locker room, personal and private. The locker itself is open, showing organised kit — operational clothing on hangers, load-bearing equipment on hooks, protective gear stacked with care. The scene is dominated by a ruggedised tactical notebook — an ATAC-style planner — sitting on a small charging dock or shelf bracket on the interior of the locker door or on an adjacent surface. A faint indicator light glows on the dock. Nearby: Peltor-style communications headset on a hook. A pair of car/vehicle keys on a separate hook. Workout gear folded on a lower shelf. A notebook and pen on a small surface. A small kit maintenance tray with brushes and cloths — the kind used for gear upkeep. A wall-mounted calendar is partially visible at the edge of frame — current month showing, no readable dates.

The ATAC on its dock is the focal point. It looks ready to be grabbed and taken out the door.

Lighting: a single overhead utility light or task lamp, warm but functional. The glow from the ATAC dock indicator adds a second subtle light point.

---

### 2. THE RANGE
**Filename:** `RANGE_HOME.png`

A tack room or working room attached to a barn or ranch outbuilding. Worn timber walls, iron hooks, the smell of the land implied by the texture of everything. The focal object: a pair of quality field glasses — binoculars — hanging on a prominent hook or resting on a worn wooden shelf, strap coiled beside them. They are the first thing your eye finds. Nearby: a set of vehicle and equipment keys on a hook. Work gloves resting on a surface. A worn almanac or land record on a shelf — spine visible, no readable title. A calendar on the wall, partially visible. A lantern or single bulb providing the only light.

The field glasses are the primary object. They look like they have been used this morning and will be used again this evening.

Lighting: pre-dawn or dusk. Warm lantern light, a thin line of natural light under a door or through a gap.

---

### 3. THE GARDEN
**Filename:** `GARDEN_HOME.png`

A potting shed or garden room, functional and loved. Timber shelving, terracotta pots, the remains of morning light through a small window. The focal object: a well-worn journal or seasonal planner, open on a potting bench — pages soft with use, a pencil resting in the spine. It looks like it was set down mid-thought and will be picked back up. Nearby: garden gloves on a hook. A set of keys hanging beside a wicker basket. Seed packets in a small wooden box. Pruning tools hung with care. A wall calendar partially visible — handwritten notes on some squares, no readable text.

The journal is the primary object. It is open and waiting.

Lighting: golden morning light through a small dusty window, falling across the bench. Warm, patient.

---

### 4. THE JOURNEY
**Filename:** `JOURNEY_HOME.png`

A small mudroom or shelter entrance — the threshold between inside and out. Timber walls, pegs on the wall, a worn bench. The focal object: a trail-worn journal or route planner, sitting open on the bench beside a packed daypack that is always ready by the door. The journal is held open by a smooth river stone. Beside it: a set of keys on a peg. A headlamp hanging on another peg. A jacket on a hook, ready. A simple calendar card or planning sheet pinned to the wall, partially visible.

The journal and the pack together form the focal point — the sense that leaving is always imminent and always prepared for.

Lighting: early morning, thin light through a low window. The outside is just visible — dark treeline, mist. The inside is warm by contrast.

---

### 5. THE PLAYBOOK
**Filename:** `PLAYBOOK_HOME.png`

A coach's office or preparation room — not a locker room, but the private space where the game is planned before anyone else arrives. A worn desk, a single task lamp on. The focal object: a coaching clipboard or ruggedised tablet on a stand or dock on the desk — play diagrams visible as abstract marks, nothing readable. Ready to be picked up. Nearby: a set of keys on the desk corner. A wall calendar with a game or event schedule visible as marks and blocks, no readable text. A coffee mug, half finished. A whiteboard on the wall behind — diagrams only, no words.

The clipboard or tablet is the focal point. The room belongs to someone who is always preparing.

Lighting: single task lamp, pre-dawn. The rest of the room in deep shadow. The lamp creates a circle of focus on the desk.

---

### 6. THE SUMMIT
**Filename:** `SUMMIT_HOME.png`

A base camp shelter interior or mountain hut — spartan, purposeful, weathered. Timber or canvas walls. The focal object: a topographic map and route card pinned to a corkboard or unfolded on a small surface — the marks of planning visible as lines and crosses, no readable text or place names. A compass rests beside it. Nearby: a set of keys or equipment clips on a hook. A headlamp. Climbing equipment — rope coiled, carabiners clipped — visible in background, not dominant. A simple calendar or weather log on the wall, partially visible.

The map and route card are the focal point — the sense that the ascent has been planned to the last detail and the plan is ready to execute.

Lighting: a single lantern or headtorch, the cold blue of altitude light through a small frosted window.

---

### 7. THE PRACTICE
**Filename:** `PRACTICE_HOME.png`

A personal training space or dojo anteroom — still, considered, nothing extraneous. Clean timber floors, shoji-influenced light from one side. The focal object: a training log or practice journal, open on a low shelf or surface at the edge of the mat — cloth-bound, pages thick with use, a brush or pen resting across it. It is the record of the work and the plan for today's work. Nearby: a set of keys on a hook. A folded training cloth or gi on a shelf. A calendar or training cycle chart on the wall — marks and cycles visible, no readable text.

The training log is the focal point. The room is prepared. The practice is about to begin.

Lighting: early morning, diffused light through shoji or linen panels. No harsh shadows. Still.

---

### 8. THE MEADOW
**Filename:** `MEADOW_HOME.png`

A small personal room or reading nook that opens onto outside — a window seat, a low table, a view of the meadow beyond. Unhurried. Nothing demands attention. The focal object: a single folded card or handwritten note on a smooth wooden surface, held in place by a smooth stone — this is the day's one true thing, present but not urgent. Nearby: a set of keys resting on a small dish. A cup of something warm. A simple wall calendar at the edge of frame — minimal, a few soft marks. Outside the window, soft morning meadow light.

The card and stone are the focal point. Everything else in the room is quiet.

Lighting: the softest in the set. Diffused morning light through linen curtains, the meadow pale gold beyond. No strong shadows. Everything at rest.
