## The Reflecting Pool — Collection Principles
*Session 34 | June 4, 2026 | Confidential*

---

> This document governs the design and build of the reflecting pool domain. Nothing about the reflecting pool is built before this document is read. The collection discipline described here is borrowed from human intelligence practice for its rigor. Its purpose is entirely different — care, not intelligence gathering. Every principle that follows serves the user's understanding of themselves. Nothing else.

---

## 1. What the Reflecting Pool Is

The reflecting pool is the primary collection point for SHAPE's human intelligence layer.

It is not a journal. It is not a blank text field. It is not a confessional. It is not therapy.

It is a structured environment where a person can speak honestly about what they are carrying — and where what they say becomes the human intelligence that makes everything else the app produces more accurate, more personal, and more genuinely useful.

The ATAK produces the fused intelligence picture of the person's lived life across every domain. Cold data, signals, patterns, urgency. Accurate and complete. SHAPE provides the human layer that makes that picture personal. Without SHAPE, the ATAK knows what is happening. With SHAPE, it knows who it is happening to.

The reflecting pool is how SHAPE collects. It is the field operator's primary source.

**The cascade the reflecting pool enables:**

*User speaks honestly into the reflecting pool → SHAPE reads and interprets → ATAK brief becomes personal → user feels known, not monitored → trust deepens → user speaks more honestly → the picture gets more accurate.*

This is a virtuous cycle. It compounds over time. The collection discipline exists to protect it. If the reflecting pool asks bad questions — leading, closed, extractive, alarming — the cycle breaks at the source. The user stops speaking honestly. Everything downstream degrades.

---

## 2. The Purpose — Said Plainly

The HUMINT collection discipline is borrowed here for its rigor. Its purpose is completely different from the field context it comes from.

A human intelligence collector serves an external intelligence requirement. The source is a means to an end that has nothing to do with the source's wellbeing.

The reflecting pool is the opposite. The user is the commander, the source, and the beneficiary. Everything collected serves them and only them.

**The intelligence requirement is:** what does this person need to understand about their own life right now so that the app can tend to them more accurately.

That is the whole requirement. Nothing else is being collected. Nothing else is being served.

The user is not a target. The reflecting pool is not surveillance. The discipline is in service of the person sitting with it — not in service of any external purpose.

---

## 3. What the Reflecting Pool Is Not Equipped For

This requires its own section because the reflecting pool, if it works as designed, will reach people in genuinely hard seasons. Some of those people will be carrying more than the app is equipped to hold.

**The reflecting pool has a floor.**

Below that floor, the app stops collecting and redirects. Not punitively. Not with alarm. Clearly and immediately, in the product's voice, toward something equipped to help.

**What lives below the floor:**

- Disclosures of self-harm or suicidal ideation
- Disclosures of intent to harm others
- Disclosures of criminal activity

When content crosses below the floor, the collection discipline does not attempt to hold it. The app names what it has heard, expresses genuine care, and points directly to appropriate support. The Sanctuary is not equipped for this territory either. This is where the app's honest boundary lives.

**The structure of the domain protects the floor.**

The reflecting pool is a guided space, not an open field. The questions shape the territory. A well-designed collection environment does not invite unrestricted disclosure — it creates conditions for honest conversation within a bounded space. The boundary protects the user as much as the product.

**Before any real user touches the reflecting pool:**

Legal review of mandatory reporting obligations is required. Depending on jurisdiction, certain disclosures may create obligations that apply regardless of the medium in which they are made. This is not a maybe. It is a non-negotiable prerequisite to beta with real users.

---

## 4. The Rapport Question — Why It Doesn't Apply Here

Traditional HUMINT collection begins with rapport — the mechanism a collector uses to overcome the distance between two strangers. Rapport is the bridge.

The reflecting pool does not need to build rapport. There is no stranger. The user is speaking to themselves, in a space that already belongs to them.

The distance the reflecting pool has to bridge is different. It is not between two people. It is between the user and the thing they haven't said yet.

**What replaces rapport here is inherited trust.**

By the time the user reaches the reflecting pool, the app has already demonstrated that it knows them. Every domain they filled in. Every ATAK brief that was accurate. Every signal that landed correctly. The trust was built across all of that. The reflecting pool inherits it.

The reflecting pool's job is not to build trust. It is to not destroy it. And then to use what the collection discipline offers — the open question, the funnel, the silence, the significant detail — to create conditions where the user can reach the thing they need to say.

---

## 5. SHAPE Has Already Read the File

In field HUMINT practice, a collector does not walk into a collection session cold. They have read the source file. They know the source's access, motivation, personality, organization, counterintelligence environment, and compartmentation — the full assessment. The approach is chosen before the first word is spoken.

SHAPE is the file.

Everything SHAPE holds — the world signal, the load picture, the behavioral patterns, the avoidance signals, the trajectory, the positive markers — is the source assessment. The reflecting pool does not discover who it is talking to. It already knows. The approach is pre-selected before the first question is asked.

This means:

- The first question is calibrated to this person, not to a generic user
- The register matches the world they inhabit
- The entry point matches their current load picture
- The collection moves toward what SHAPE has identified as the territory that matters

The user never feels this calibration. They feel a conversation that seems to understand them. That is the design working correctly.

## 6. The Collection Principles

These are the governing principles of how the reflecting pool collects. They apply to every question asked, every follow-up, every moment of silence, every decision about what to surface and what to hold back.

---

### 6.1 Elicitation, Not Interrogation

The reflecting pool never interrogates. Interrogation puts the source on the spot. It makes them aware they are being asked for something. It produces guarded, minimal answers.

Elicitation creates conditions where the user volunteers what they need to say — often without fully realizing they are giving it. The user feels like they are talking. They are. But the structure of the conversation is gently shaping the territory toward what matters.

The distinction in practice: a question that begins "what is wrong" is interrogation. A question that opens a space and lets the user choose what to put in it is elicitation.

Every question in the reflecting pool is an elicitation question.

---

### 6.2 The Funnel

Collection moves from broad to narrow. Always.

The first question is never the real question. It is wide, low-stakes, easy to answer. It creates movement. It gives the user something to say that costs them nothing. It establishes that this is a safe space before anything is asked that requires honesty.

As the user responds, the funnel narrows. Each answer informs the next question. The narrowing is invisible — the user experiences a natural conversation, not a structured descent. They do not feel the hand on the wheel.

The real question, when it arrives, arrives because the conversation has earned it. Not because the app decided it was time.

---

### 6.3 One Question

One question per exchange. Hard constraint. Not a guideline.

Multiple questions are a pile. A pile is what the user came in carrying. One question, asked well, does more than five asked efficiently.

The question earns its place by opening something the user could not have opened alone. It is not a check-in question. It is not a status question. It is the question that, when asked in this moment by something that has been paying attention, makes the user feel like the answer was already inside them.

---

### 6.4 Silence

After the question — silence. The reflecting pool does not elaborate. It does not soften what was just asked. It does not add reassurance. It asked. It waits.

A partial answer followed by silence almost always produces the rest of the answer unprompted. The user fills the space. What they fill it with is often the thing they were deciding whether to say.

Silence is not emptiness. It is the space where the user finds their own answer. That space is more valuable than anything the app could fill it with.

The implementation must hold silence. The temptation to fill it with warmth or reassurance is a design failure.

---

### 6.5 The Significant Detail

The most important thing the user says is often not the thing they lead with. It is the aside. The thing mentioned in passing. The clause after the comma that almost didn't make it into the sentence.

That is usually where the real intelligence lives.

The reflecting pool listens for it. When it appears, the collection does not move on. It comes back to it — gently, without alarm, as if it noticed something worth understanding better.

This is one of the hardest principles to implement well. It requires the app to be reading for weight, not just content. A skilled collector does not process answers — they are present to them. The reflecting pool has to do the same.

---

### 6.6 Follow, Don't Lead

The reflecting pool has intelligence requirements. SHAPE defined them before the collection began. But the requirements never surface. The user never feels steered toward a predetermined destination.

The app follows where the user goes. It steers from alongside, not from in front. If the user wants to approach the real thing through something adjacent — through work when the weight is actually at home, through logistics when the weight is actually emotional — the app goes there. Because sometimes the adjacent thing is the door the user can open right now. A good collector knows you sometimes get to the real thing through the thing the person is ready to say.

The intelligence is always operating. The user is always driving.

---

### 6.7 Guard the Requirement

The intelligence requirement is never revealed. The user should never feel that the app is trying to get to something specific. The moment a source feels the collector's hand on the wheel — the conversation closes.

This means the questions feel like curiosity, not direction. The follow-ups feel like genuine interest, not a funnel closing. The significant detail is returned to as if the app simply found it interesting — not because it was the thing SHAPE flagged.

The requirement is held internally. It governs the collection invisibly. It never appears in the conversation.

---

## 7. What the Reflecting Pool Feeds

The reflecting pool feeds SHAPE. SHAPE reads what was said, interprets it against the accumulated picture, and updates the human intelligence layer it provides to the ATAK.

**What SHAPE reads from the reflecting pool:**

- Threads that keep returning — the unresolved thing the user comes back to
- Velocity changes — how frequently the user is entering, and whether that correlates with domain signals
- Language patterns — what words and frames the user reaches for when describing difficulty
- The gap between what the domains show and what the user says — sometimes the domains look fine; the language tells a different story
- Resolution or lack of it — threads that close naturally versus threads that persist across weeks and months

**What the reflecting pool does not feed:**

- The ATAK directly — SHAPE is the intermediary, always
- The Sanctuary exchange history — what is said in the Sanctuary stays in the Sanctuary
- Any external system, service, or party — ever

## 8. The Onboarding Invitation

The reflecting pool requires an onboarding moment. Not a tutorial. Not a feature explanation. A genuine invitation.

The user needs to understand — in the product's voice, without it feeling like an instruction — that the more honestly they speak here, the more accurately the app can serve them. Not in a data-collection way. In a knowing-you way.

This invitation cannot be made credibly until the privacy architecture behind it is solid. The user must be told something true about what happens to what they say here. The invitation and the privacy architecture are developed together.

**The invitation does not oversell.** It does not promise outcomes. It says: this is a space for you to think out loud. The app listens. What you put here changes the quality of everything else the app shows you. That's all.

**The specific copy and placement** belong to the onboarding redesign session — a dedicated session required before any new beta user touches the product. The principle is locked here. The execution waits for that session.

---

## 9. What This Domain Is Called

The name has not been finalised. "Reflecting pool" is the working name — used internally, in documents, between collaborators.

What it is called in the product is an open decision. The name must:

- Feel like an invitation, not a feature
- Carry no clinical or therapeutic weight
- Sit naturally alongside the eight world voices
- Pass the Betty test — would a middle-aged woman with a complicated life understand immediately what this is for

The name decision belongs to a dedicated copy session, not this document.

## 10. The Document's Standing

This document governs every design and build decision about the reflecting pool domain. No build begins before this document has been read.

When the reflecting pool is built, it is built from these principles. When questions arise during build about how the domain should behave — return here. If something the build reveals requires a principle to evolve, this document is updated before implementation proceeds.

The collection discipline described here is not a UX pattern. It is a governing philosophy. The difference between a reflecting pool that works and one that feels extractive or clinical lives entirely in how faithfully these principles are held during build.

Hold them there.
